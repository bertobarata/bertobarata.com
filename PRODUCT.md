# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences land on bertobarata.com, and the site serves both at once:

1. **Prospective clients** (small businesses, local services, associations, professionals) who need a website and are deciding whether to trust Berto with it. They arrive from a referral, an Instagram bio, or a Barata Studio link. Context: quick evaluation, often on mobile, low patience for fluff. Job to be done: "convince me you do real, careful work and are easy to talk to."

2. **Professional contacts** (recruiters, collaborators, partners) evaluating technical credibility. Context: skimming for competence signals, stack, real projects. Job to be done: "is this person a capable engineer and designer, not just a template flipper?"

Both should leave able to see the work, understand who Berto is, and reach him in one step.

## Product Purpose

A single personal home for Berto Barata that closes the gap between "I saw your name" and "I trust you enough to contact you." It presents real delivered work (client websites, live and linkable), the person behind it (founder of Barata Studio, engineering student, financial mediator, father), and a direct line to talk. Success = a qualified visitor reaches out, or a professional contact walks away convinced of competence. It is a proof surface, not a brochure.

## Brand Personality

Bold, technical, distinctive. The site commits to a strong point of view rather than playing safe: display type as a statement (dot-matrix wordmark), a load-bearing signature color, deliberate motion. Confident and authorial, it lets the work carry the claim without hype. A real person stays present underneath the boldness (the humour in "Pai, o meu melhor projeto", the honesty of the journey), so the strength reads as craft and personality, not as cold spectacle. Three words: bold, technical, distinctive.

## Anti-references

- **Generic template portfolio.** Bought theme, Wix-shaped, identical to a thousand others. If it could belong to anyone, it has failed.
- **Cold corporate SaaS landing.** Navy gradients, hero-metric row, stock photography, buzzword copy, no human behind it.
- **Boring CV.** Dry chronological list, a PDF rendered in a browser, zero design intent.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over promise.** Show real projects with live links, not adjectives about skill. The work is the argument.
2. **Made by hand, and it shows.** Every detail is deliberate. The craft itself is the differentiator from templates.
3. **Committed, not timid.** A strong point of view beats safe restraint. The bold typographic and color choices are deliberate, load-bearing, and consistent, so the site reads as designed rather than defaulted. Distinctiveness is the competence signal.
4. **Human, not cold.** A real person is behind this (studio, engineering, mediation, fatherhood). Warmth without pose, so it never becomes another faceless SaaS page.
5. **One voice, many fronts.** Dev, studio, and financial mediation cohere under a single identity rather than reading as three disconnected personas.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Text contrast holds against the dark surface and the lime accent (accent used on dark, not as small body text). Motion (rotating roles, scroll cue, reveals) is decorative and fully disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard focus and semantic landmarks preserved. Portuguese primary language, plain and direct copy.
